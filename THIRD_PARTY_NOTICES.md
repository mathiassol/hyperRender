## Test content (not in this repository)

`tools/fetch_assets.ps1` downloads test scenes into `assets/downloaded/` (git-ignored) for
the examples and the regression tests. They aren't redistributed here; each keeps its own
license, listed beside it in the download:

- [Khronos glTF Sample Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets): Sponza
  (Crytek's, under the CRYENGINE limited license agreement), DamagedHelmet, CesiumMan,
  AlphaBlendModeTest and EnvironmentTest (CC BY 4.0 or similar, per model).
- [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/) HDRIs: CC0.

`assets/scenes/cornell_box.gltf` is generated by `tools/make_cornell_box.py` and is part of
this repository under its MIT license.
